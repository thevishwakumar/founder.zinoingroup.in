## 4. Visual Identity System & Design Standards

### 4.1 Corporate Brand Palette
* **Primary Brand Blue (`#2563eb` / `#3b82f6`):** Communicates technical authority, clarity, and reliability.
* **Deep Slate Navy (`#0f172a` / `#090d16`):** Grounded executive surface tone reflecting institutional stability.
* **Accent Indigo (`#6366f1`):** Represents innovation and forward-looking digital ventures.
* **Surface Tones (`#f8fafc` / `#111827`):** High-contrast background surfaces engineered for WCAG AAA legibility.

### 4.2 Typography Hierarchy
* **Headings:** `Outfit`, sans-serif (Geometric, executive, modern, high-impact weight 700/800).
* **Body Prose:** `Inter`, sans-serif (Industry-standard UI legibility, neutral, optimized optical sizing).

### 4.3 Asset Usage Guidelines
* **Corporate Logo:** Always use the official Zinoin Group corporate emblem (`assets/img/zinoin-logo.webp` / `.png`). Never replace with arbitrary generated text or unbranded icons.
* **Founder Portrait:** Use the verified executive portrait (`assets/img/about.webp` / `.jpg`). Do not substitute with stock photography.

## 6. Reputation & Fact Governance

* **Zero Fabricated Claims:** All claims regarding education, metrics, clients, and certifications are strictly validated against verifiable evidence.
* **Geographic Accuracy:** Outdated Bangalore residential references were completely purged; geographic data is strictly anchored to Coimbatore (college) and Krishnagiri (school/hometown).
