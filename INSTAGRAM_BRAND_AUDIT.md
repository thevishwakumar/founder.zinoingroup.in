## 6. Website Technical Safeguards (Performance & Privacy)

### Why Direct Instagram Embeds Are Excluded From the Founder Website
1. **Performance Preservation:** An embedded Instagram widget injects over **800 KB of third-party JavaScript**, stylesheets, and tracking cookies, which would immediately degrade the website's **100/100 Lighthouse performance** and introduce Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
2. **User Privacy & GDPR/DPDP Compliance:** Meta embed scripts track user IP addresses and browsing habits. Maintaining direct, static anchor links (`<a href="https://www.instagram.com/thevishwakumar/">`) respects visitor privacy and avoids compliance liabilities.
3. **Design Purity:** External iframes break the custom dark navy corporate design system established in `styles.css`.
