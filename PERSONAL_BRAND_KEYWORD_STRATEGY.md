## 5. Ethical Guidelines & Quality Controls

1. **Zero Keyword Stuffing:** Keywords appear only where they make grammatical and semantic sense to human readers.
2. **Zero False Claims:** No claims of unearned senior titles, venture capital funding, or enterprise awards.
3. **No Hidden Text:** Zero CSS text concealment (`display: none`, off-screen text, zero opacity text) used for keyword injection.
4. **Authentic Synonyms:** Natural variations ("Founder & Developer", "Founder and Developer", "Vishwa K", "Vishwa Kumar") used in appropriate contexts.
